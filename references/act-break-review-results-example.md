# Act Break Full-Stack Review — Example

**Reviewed:** Chapters 1-10 (~27,500 words)
**Reviewers:** Alex (Developmental), Jordan (Line), Casey (Continuity), Quinn (Fact Check), Morgan (Beta Reader)

## What This Document Is

A worked example of the Stage 5c Act Break Full-Stack Review showing the kinds of cross-chapter issues this process catches, how findings are consolidated, and how fixes are applied directly to chapter files.

## Findings Consolidated

### Alex (Developmental) — Structure & Pacing

1. **Act Two ramp starts too early.**
   - Chapter 7 has high-tension material that belongs in Act Two's escalation. The pacing arc needs a slower burn through Act One's setup phase.
   - **Fix:** Downgrade tension in Ch7 — replace threat sequence with character-building scene. One cross-chapter POV shift moved to Ch9.

2. **Protagonist's emotional arc beats are uneven.**
   - Chapters 2-4 have dense emotional beats, then Chapters 5-8 shift entirely to plot mechanics. The emotional through-line gets lost.
   - **Fix:** Added two internal-marker paragraphs in Ch6 and Ch8 reconnecting to protagonist's emotional state.

### Jordan (Line) — Prose Quality & Voice

1. **POV voice drift in Chapter 5.**
   - The prose in Ch5 is more formal/detached than the established POV voice in Ch1-4. Reads like a different narrator.
   - **Fix:** Rewrote three paragraphs in Ch5 to match voice guide: shorter sentences, active voice, character-specific word choices.

2. **Weak sentence endings in transition paragraphs.**
   - Scene transitions across all chapters end with trailing, low-energy sentences that deflate tension.
   - **Fix:** Tightened 12 transition sentences across Ch1-10 — cut qualifiers, end on concrete images or decisions.

3. **Dialogue attribution redundancy.**
   - Pattern of "he said, making a point of..." follows dialogue across multiple chapters.
   - **Fix:** Cut 14 redundant attributions. Let dialogue stand on its own where the speaker is clear.

### Casey (Continuity) — Timeline & Character Consistency

1. **Timeline math error.**
   - Ch3: "three days after the attack" — Ch7: "a week passed" — but only 2 chapters (2 in-story days) separate them. Total elapsed time is 5 days, not 10.
   - **Fix:** Ch7 changed to "a few days passed." Full timeline recalculated and cross-referenced.

2. **Character detail contradiction.**
   - Ch2: Protagonist is left-handed (pours coffee with left hand). Ch6: Shakes hands with right — fine, but Ch9: Fires weapon left-handed after claiming to be ambidextrous in Ch3. No ambidexterity was established.
   - **Fix:** Added one line to Ch3 mentioning weapons training as ambidextrous. Removed the telling, kept the showing.

3. **Seeded thread lost.**
   - Ch4 established a key item that was meant to pay off in Ch10. The payoff is absent — the item is never mentioned again.
   - **Fix:** Added a reference to the item in Ch10's climax scene. The character now sees it, creating the intended callback.

### Quinn (Fact-Check) — Technical Accuracy

1. **Terminology error across Ch2 and Ch6.**
   - Described a communications protocol that doesn't exist in the way portrayed. The technical detail is used twice, both times wrong.
   - **Fix:** Replaced with correct protocol name and realistic description. Cross-referenced with world outline.

### Morgan (Beta Reader) — Immersion & Engagement

1. **Chapter 4 exposition dump.**
   - Six paragraphs of backstory delivered as an info-dump. Killed momentum at a critical point.
   - **Fix:** Spread backstory across three earlier chapters as organic references. Ch4 now opens with action, then one paragraph of relevant backstory.

2. **Ch9 fight scene loses spatial tracking.**
   - Reader can't follow who is where. Characters reference positions not established in the scene setup.
   - **Fix:** Added two lines of spatial setup at scene start. Trimmed redundant action beats.

## Items Requiring Author Decision

1. **Supporting character name change.** Casey flagged that two supporting characters have similar-sounding names starting with the same letter. Could confuse readers. Options: rename one or keep (they appear in different scenes).
2. **Time compression in Act One.** Alex's pacing fix compresses the timeline. Author should confirm whether 5 days works for the story's internal logic.

## Lessons for Future Batches

- **Do a Casey pass mid-batch** for timeline math — don't wait for act break
- **Terminology errors compound** when two chapters use the same wrong detail — catch on the first occurrence
- **Voice drift between POV chapters** is the most common issue in parallel writing — tighten Jordan's review criteria for batch mode
