# Act-Break to Next-Chapter Handoff

Use this when a continuity / act-break review is required before drafting the next chapter, especially under standing auto-approval.

## Required sequence

1. Run the act/continuity review before the next chapter review or approval.
2. Use the normal review artifact format and write a file such as `reviews/act-2a-continuity-act-break-review.md`.
3. Required reviewer set for an act-break continuity gate should include the continuity/structure/plausibility stack: Casey, Alex, Morgan, Hayes, Vale, Jordan; add Quinn when safety/fact-boundary issues are active.
4. Apply actionable fixes immediately, even if small. Example: remove unsupported numeric specificity rather than carrying a dangling continuity claim forward.
5. Update the review's `Fixes Applied` section with actual changes made.
6. Update `progress.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and `rolling-summary.md` before drafting the next chapter.
7. If standing auto-approval is active and no author-only decision remains, continue directly into the next chapter rather than stopping at the review.

## Carry-forward controls to record

Every act-break review should end with concrete controls for the next chapter:

- who owns the next scene's agency / POV burden
- time window and physical state continuity
- knowledge-state labels: verified, unverified, restricted, caveated, public, private
- artifact custody: originals vs marked working copies
- institutional constraints: who can argue, approve, coordinate, or refuse
- safety boundaries for any cyber/grid/hostage/action material
- prose/style risks to monitor in the next chapter

## Pitfalls

- Do not let a review-only finding remain abstract. Convert it into either a text fix, a tracking-file update, or a named carry-forward control.
- Do not report the act-break review as complete until the review file exists and tracking files reflect the new baseline.
- Do not let chapter prose leak process labels such as `Act 2B`, `Chapter 21`, or reviewer/persona language. These are workflow labels, not story-world language.
- Do not let a medically or institutionally constrained civilian drift back into staff role merely because the next chapter needs exposition. Shift burden to the POV character who owns the chapter.
