# Baltic Intelligence Services — Quick Reference

Use this when writing intelligence scenes involving Baltic states. These are the primary agencies that would interact with the British Embassy crisis cell or serve as ground-level sources.

## Lithuania

| Acronym | Full Name | English | Role |
|---------|-----------|---------|------|
| VSD | Valstybės saugumo departamentas | State Security Department | Domestic intelligence / counter-intelligence |
| AOTD | Antrasis operatyvinių tarnybų departamentas (prie Krašto apsaugos ministerijos) | Second Operational Research Department (under Ministry of National Defence) | Military intelligence |

## Common Pitfalls

- **Do not invent fictional expansions.** VSD ≠ "Victim Support Desk." AOTD ≠ "Allied Operational Threat Detection." These are real Lithuanian intelligence agencies with established Lithuanian-language names.
- **Do not use NATO-style acronyms** for Baltic services unless they are actual NATO designations. VSD and AOTD are native Lithuanian abbreviations.
- **The relay mechanism** for hostage observations reaching a crisis cell is typically through the Embassy liaison channel (Defence Attaché → local intelligence service), not a direct humanitarian channel.
- **ORCON** (Originator-Controlled) is the correct NATO dissemination control marking. Not fictional.

## Usage in Bikini Black

- VSD/AOTD provide the relay channel for Helen Crowther's captivity observations (Ch 20, Act 3).
- The relay arrives via Embassy liaison, not direct contact — preserves Helen's operational security.
- SKW (Kamiński's agency) is Polish, not Lithuanian — ensure jurisdictional separation is maintained.
