# Stage 13 — Developmental Edit Handoff

Use this after Stage 11 continuity and Stage 12 full-stack review have passed and the project advances to developmental edit.

## Purpose

Stage 13 is a macro structural decision pass, not a reflexive rewrite pass. The goal is to decide whether the manuscript needs structural repair before line editing.

## Required Inputs

- `progress.md` current state
- `AGENTS.md` current state and carry-forward constraints
- `rolling-summary.md`
- Stage 11 continuity / mission-logic review
- Stage 12 full-stack review
- Weak-spot chapters named by Stage 12
- Relevant outline / scene-breakdown / story-structure excerpts

## Required Reviewers

- Alex primary
- Morgan support
- Casey support
- Hayes support
- Vale support

Add Jordan for prose/line-edit handoff and Quinn for safety/fact-check boundary when the manuscript includes technical, military, intelligence, hostage, or infrastructure material.

## Decision Standard

Ask whether any of these are required before line edit:

- structural rewrite
- chapter reorder
- character-arc rebuild
- mission-logic repair
- ending replacement
- new scene / deleted scene
- targeted expansion of an underweighted chapter

If the answer is no, say so plainly and do not edit chapters just to prove work happened. Convert the issue into a Stage 14/15 carry-forward item.

## Common Pattern: Lean Final-Act Bridge Chapters

If Stage 12 flags lean bridge chapters before the climax, inspect them against function, not word count:

- Do they orient the reader?
- Do they escalate pressure?
- Do they clarify authority lanes / mission state?
- Do they accelerate into the climax?
- Would expansion add necessary texture or just slow momentum?

If leanness supports acceleration, retain it and record the decision in the Stage 13 review.

## Output

Write `reviews/stage-13-developmental-edit.md` with:

- gate status
- verification snapshot
- developmental edit decisions table
- reviewer sections
- fixes applied / decisions made
- author decisions needed
- dissent / unresolved concerns
- next required step

## Tracking Updates

After review passes:

- Mark Stage 13 complete in `progress.md` and `AGENTS.md`.
- Set current stage / next required action to Stage 14 — Line Editing.
- Add Stage 13 audit state to `rolling-summary.md`.
- Verify chapter count, review count, Stage 13 review file presence, review gate, missing reviewers none, and manuscript word total.

## Pitfalls

- Do not make line edits during Stage 13 unless a structural issue requires a manuscript edit.
- Do not expand chapters solely because they are shorter than earlier chapters.
- Preserve hard continuity constraints while handing off to line edit: hidden-institution opacity, unresolved statuses, civilian-adviser boundaries, and consequence-level safety framing.
- Carry exact-title / terminology uncertainties to Stage 15 fact-check instead of solving them with guesses.
