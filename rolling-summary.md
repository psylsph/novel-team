# Rolling Summary

**Purpose:** A concise, up-to-date summary of what has happened in the story so far. Updated after every approved chapter.

**Rules:**
- Keep it concise — focus on plot events, character state changes, and established facts
- Do NOT include prose, dialogue, or scene description — only what happened
- Update after every chapter is approved (passes all review gates)
- Remove details that are no longer relevant (older events that don't affect current plot threads)
- Keep character states current (where they are, what they know, what they want)
- Track active plot threads and unresolved questions
- Note relationships and dynamics that have evolved
