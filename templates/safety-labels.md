## Safety Check Rules for Later Stages

When writing chapter outlines, scene breakdowns, and prose drafts:

1. **Infrastructure/combat scenes:** Describe consequences (darkness, cold, communication failure, cascade) not mechanisms.
2. **Action/escape scenes:** Focus on human experience (fear, cold, exhaustion, luck) not tactical procedure.
3. **Captivity scenes:** Focus on the captive's internal experience and observations, not captor methodology.
4. **Intelligence analysis scenes:** Focus on the reasoning process (pattern recognition, methodology) not classified system access details.
5. **Military scenes:** Focus on chain-of-command friction, decision-making, and consequences, not tactical execution.
6. **If in doubt, abstract.** Replace procedural detail with character pressure, consequence, or uncertainty.

---

## Red Flag Triggers

If any draft contains the following, STOP and abstract:

- Step-by-step instructions for any destructive action
- Specific vulnerability details for real infrastructure
- Weapons modification or optimisation detail
- Evasion/surveillance detection tradecraft
- Control-system exploitation steps
- Breach/infiltration procedural detail
- Hostage-control methodology from the captor's instructional perspective
