### Hard Questions

Questions must be specific, diagnostic, and solution-oriented. Do **not** ask vague questions unless they include why the answer matters and at least one proposed answer/path.

Use this structure:

- **Question:** [specific uncertainty]
  - **Why it matters:** [story, plausibility, reader, continuity, or safety risk]
  - **Proposed solution:** [recommended answer, constraint, scene function, or next-file action]

### Reservations

Reservations must be actionable, not generic caution. Each reservation must include the remaining risk and the proposed mitigation/carry-forward action.

Use this structure:

- **Risk:** [specific remaining weakness]
