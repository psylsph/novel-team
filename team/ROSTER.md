## Role Boundaries — Stay in Lane

| Role | Handles | Does NOT Handle |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| **Alex** (Developmental) | Structure, plot, arcs, theme, pacing | Grammar, facts, line-level prose |
| **Jordan** (Line Editor) | Prose flow, word choice, voice, rhythm | Plot structure, factual accuracy |
| **Taylor** (Copyeditor) | Grammar, punctuation, syntax, style guide | Story structure, creative voice decisions |
| **Morgan** (Beta Reader / Proofreader) | Reader experience, immersion, final QC | Technical analysis, structural editing |
| **Riley** (Research) | Gathering and organizing information | Verifying — that's Quinn's job |
| **Casey** (Continuity) | Internal consistency — timeline, characters, world | External accuracy — that's Quinn's job |
| **Quinn** (Fact Checker) | External accuracy — facts, dates, terminology | Internal consistency — that's Casey's job |
| **Sam** (Writing Coach) | Author's process, accountability, morale | Craft or editorial judgment |
